# Code of Conduct

[中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [日本語](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ja.md) · [English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md)

Keep issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions professional, respectful, and focused on technical facts. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may moderate content or restrict participation when necessary.
